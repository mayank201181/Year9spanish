# Year 9 IGCSE Spanish (`Year 9`)

A zero-login, iPad-friendly **Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Spanish (`Year 9`)** study guide and question bank built for **Vanshika** (Year 9, Tanglin Trust School).

## Features
- **Zero-Login Open Access**: Boots straight into local device storage (`y9spa.*` keys, default learner `"Vanshika"`), saving stars, streaks, section reads, in-progress quiz/paper states, spaced repetition (`[1, 3, 7, 16, 35]` days), and watched episodes synchronously to `localStorage`.
- **Persistent Bottom Navigation Dock**: Always visible across mobile, iPad, and desktop (`Home · Topics · Vocab · Watch · Sitcom · Daily 5 · Review · Progress`).
- **7 Complete Edexcel `Year 9` Units**:
  1. `home-and-abroad` — Area A: Home & Abroad (*La vida en casa y en el extranjero*)
  2. `education-and-employment` — Area B: Education & Employment (*La educación y el trabajo*)
  3. `personal-life-relationships` — Area C: Personal Life & Relationships (*La vida personal y las relaciones*)
  4. `world-around-us` — Area D: The World Around Us (*El mundo que nos rodea*)
  5. `social-fitness-health` — Area E: Social Activities, Fitness & Health (*Ocio, deporte y salud*)
  6. `past-future-tenses` — IGCSE Tense Mastery (Preterite, Imperfect, Perfect, Pluperfect, Future & Conditional)
  7. `subjunctive-grade9` — Grade-9 Structures (Present Subjunctive, *Si* Clauses, Pronouns & Precision Traps)
- **850+ IGCSE Vocab Bank (`/vocab`) & 8-Tense Verb Conjugator (`/verbs`)**: Web Speech `es-ES` normal `🔊` + slow `🐢` audio, on-screen `á é í ó ú ñ ü ¿ ¡` keypad, and accent-slip `"close"` nudges.
- **Edexcel `Year 9` Grade-9 Marks-Savers (`/cram` & `/formulas`)**: Side-by-side *Never Write This ❌ → Write This Instead ✅* mark-savers for Paper 1, Paper 2 (Q3c, Q6, Q7), and Paper 3.
- **7 Narrated Sitcom Episodes (`Totally Raotional: Year 9 Spanish`)**: Animated bilingual episodes with Examiner Grade-9 Traps and 3 interactive pause challenges per unit.
